## Brand & Style

This design system blends academic rigor and foundational heritage with the pace and clarity of high-conversion digital learning platforms. The brand voice balances scholarly authority with radical accessibility, tailored for international adult learners, certificate seekers, and modern ministry practitioners.

The design movement combines **Contemporary EdTech Minimalism** with **Editorial Academic Precision**:
- High-contrast, spacious typography framing career-outcome statistics.
- Purposeful warmth derived from terracotta and cocoa tones, rejecting cold institutional aesthetics.
- Structured card grids and dynamic progression micro-indicators influenced by modular educational platforms.

## Colors

The palette grounds high-energy conversion cues within earthy, theological stability:
- **Primary Terracotta (`#E06A26`)**: Reserved for core conversion targets, primary action buttons, and active progression paths.
- **Secondary Cocoa Bark (`#5C3317`)**: Anchor tone for hero surfaces, authoritative badges, strong header typography, and footer modules.
- **Tertiary Golden Amber (`#EBB328`)**: Focus indicator, distinction banners, scholarship badges, and star ratings.
- **Neutral Slate (`#64748B`)**: Body text secondary tier, metadata, icon outlines, and secondary system borders.
- **Surface Strategy**: The primary canvas is Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`). Warm Cream (`#FFFBF5`) designates featured program containers and credential previews, while Ice Slate (`#F8FAFC`) separates structural curriculum modules.

## Typography

The type scale orchestrates a high-performing pedagogical experience:
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides punchy, geometric headlines and scannable metrics that replicate modern corporate venture bootcamps.
- **Inter** ensures uninterrupted long-form reading across theological doctrine, syllabi, course outcomes, and legal prerequisites.
- Metrics, statistics, and impact counters use tight kerning and dense line heights (`stat-display`) to drive proof-point credibility immediately.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column grid is maintained across desktop (breakpoint: 1200px+) with 24px gutters, transitioning to 8 columns on tablet (768px - 1199px) and a fluid 4-column layout on mobile (<768px).

Content follows rhythmic vertical blocks:
- **Application Flow & Syllabi**: Bound within an 1140px maximum content width to preserve reading cadence.
- **Program Grids & Course Catalog**: Full-width fluid container with a 1320px maximum constraint, anchoring multi-tier credential tracks.
- Structural transitions leverage generous block padding (`space-2xl`) between sections to provide breathing room typical of executive educational publications.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows in favor of a layered, tactile realism driven by soft warmth:
- **Base Level (Resting Card)**: Bound by crisp, 1px low-contrast borders (`#EBE3DA` or `#E2E8F0`) with a faint tinted ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(92, 51, 23, 0.04)`.
- **Active / Interactive Lift**: On hover, interactive cards elevate with a smooth transition (`transition: transform 200ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), box-shadow 200ms ease`) applying `transform: translateY(-3px)` and `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(92, 51, 23, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(92, 51, 23, 0.04)`.
- **Flyouts & Modals**: Layered over deep cocoa glass scrim (`rgba(30, 18, 10, 0.6)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`) backed by `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(30, 18, 10, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The roundedness level sits at `2` (medium-soft architecture):
- Standard interactive elements (buttons, inputs, notification pills) use `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius.
- Cards, modules, curriculum stages, and dialogs inherit `1rem` (16px) radius (`rounded-lg`).
- Metric badges, integration chips, and cohort countdowns use full pill profiles (`9999px`) to create distinction against rectilinear course structures.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Enrollment CTA**: Background `#E06A26`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold. Subtle inset glow `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. On hover: `#D05C18` with 200ms lift.
- **Secondary Inquire CTA**: Background transparent, border 1.5px solid `#5C3317`, text `#5C3317`. On hover: Background `#5C3317`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Tertiary Link**: Clean inline text with 2px underline in `#E06A26`, `text-underline-offset: 4px`.

### Metric Pills & Accreditation Chips
- Stat and cohort pills utilize a solid pill form factor (`rounded-full`), padded `space-xs` vertically by `space-md` horizontally.
- Background uses subtle amber wash (`rgba(235, 179, 40, 0.12)`), text `#5C3317`, accompanied by bold status micro-dots.

### Google Classroom Integration Tags
- Specific integration badges incorporate a crisp `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC` background with a subtle `#E2E8F0` border.
- Features the Google Classroom green indicator (`#1E8E3E`) along with a bold label `Classroom Integrated`, set in `label-code` uppercase.

### Cards & Program Containers
- **Standard Course Card**: Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, 24px padding (`space-lg`), containing title, credit hours, duration pill, and dual CTA cluster.
- **Featured / Degree Pathway Card**: Background `#FFFBF5`, 1.5px border `#E06A26`, corner-positioned ribbon for priority programs, incorporating deep cocoa headline typography (`#5C3317`).

### Input Fields & Application Steps
- Fields feature 1px solid border `#CBD5E1`, internal padding 12px 16px, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#E06A26` with a warm glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(224, 106, 38, 0.15)`). No harsh default browser rings.

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- Custom controls using `#5C3317` unchecked borders, filling to `#E06A26` when active, with high-contrast white checkmarks and radio pips.
